- Baby pink, lavender, butter yellow, peach, mint green, and creamy white.
- Cute flowers, tiny stars, butterflies, sparkles, hearts, clouds, and little moons.
- Traditional Maharashtrian festive decorations, floral garlands, rangoli, and torans.
- Soft rounded shapes, delicate shadows, cute illustrations, and beautiful Hindi typography.

The design must be adorable, elegant, culturally respectful, and visually delightful for children.

Add a cute baby-themed illustration, floating hearts, butterflies, and twinkling stars.

“प्यार से निमंत्रण खोलें 💌”
